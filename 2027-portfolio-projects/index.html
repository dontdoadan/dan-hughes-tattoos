How the project works

Time to finish it properly.

The purpose of a fixed project price is to give both sides clarity. You know the agreed price; I can give the original brief the time it needs.

01

The agreed scope is protected

We agree the design direction, body area, placement and coverage before the project begins. That becomes the scope of the commission.

02

Completion time is included

If the original agreed tattoo needs additional tattooing time to reach the intended finish, that time is included in the fixed project price.

03

Changes are agreed separately

New coverage, a different body area, a replacement concept or substantial client-requested changes are outside the original scope and quoted separately before work proceeds.

04

50% deposit to secure

A 50% deposit forms part of the fixed total and is due at least seven days before the first session. It is not an additional fee.

05

Balance paid across the project

The remaining balance can be paid in agreed instalments. All payments are credited to the fixed price and the full balance is due by completion.

06

Photography is separate

Any photography or video permission is requested separately. Applying does not give permission to publish your images or tattoo.

Questions, answered

Before you apply.

Everything important about the selected project structure, price and application process.

What does the fixed project price include?

It includes the agreed tattoo design direction, placement and coverage, plus the tattooing time reasonably needed to complete that original agreed scope. The minimum days shown are estimates, not a limit on the time included.

What is not included?

Additional coverage, a different body area, a replacement concept or substantial client-requested changes to the approved scope. If you want to change the brief, the additional work will be discussed and priced separately before it proceeds.

How does the deposit work?

A 50% deposit is required to secure a selected project and must be paid at least seven days before the first session. It forms part of the fixed total. The remaining balance is agreed in advance and must be paid by project completion.

Will I be charged if the tattoo takes longer?

Not if the additional time is needed to complete the original agreed scope. That is included in the fixed project price. Additional work outside that scope is not.

Do I have to choose one of the ten concepts?

No. You can select a concept as a starting point or choose “open to a recommendation”. The final composition is developed around your brief, placement and anatomy.

Does applying give permission to use my tattoo in marketing?

No. Reference images are used only to assess your application. Any photography or video of your tattoo, and any use in a portfolio or marketing, requires separate permission with the intended channels explained.

When will I know if I have been selected?

Applications are reviewed individually. If your project is a fit, I’ll contact you to discuss the brief and next steps. An application is not a confirmed booking, and a place is not reserved until the written agreement and deposit arrangements are completed.
