Questions, answered

BEFORE YOU APPLY.

Are the prices fixed?

The listed prices are the selected fixed prices for the quarter, half and full sleeve categories, subject to the agreed design, placement and coverage. The convention project's fixed price is confirmed before booking.

What happens if the tattoo takes longer?

If additional tattooing time is needed to complete the original agreed design to the intended standard, that time is included in the selected sleeve project price. This does not cover new coverage, a replacement concept or substantial changes to the agreed brief.

How does the booking deposit work?

The booking deposit is £200 for a quarter sleeve, £300 for a half sleeve and £400 for a full sleeve. The convention project also has a £400 booking deposit unless its agreed scope requires a different amount. Your deposit secures the project and first appointment, rolls forward throughout the project and is deducted from your final session. It is not charged again. Each subsequent session is paid separately, either beforehand or on the day of your appointment.

Do I have to choose one of the concepts?

No. The categories describe the available project places. The final design is developed with you around your idea, anatomy and placement.

Does applying give permission to use my tattoo in your portfolio?

No. Photography or video permission is requested separately. Applying does not give permission to publish your tattoo or personal images.

Does an application guarantee a place?

No. Applications are reviewed individually. A place is only confirmed after selection, written agreement and deposit arrangements.
