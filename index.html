02 / The approach

Not off
the shelf.

Strong tattoos are the outcome of strong planning. From initial direction to the final session, every decision serves the finished piece.

01 /

Discuss
the idea

Share what you have in mind — the reference imagery, placement, scale and meaning. We establish whether it is a good fit for the studio.

02 /

Build the
composition

Develop the design around the shape and movement of the body, keeping the whole piece in view instead of treating each detail in isolation.

03 /

Make it
permanent

Structure the sessions and give each stage the time required for detailed, considered tattooing.
