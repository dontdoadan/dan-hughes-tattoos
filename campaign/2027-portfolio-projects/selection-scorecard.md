# Selection Scorecard

## Weighted criteria

Rate each criterion from 1 to 5. The database calculates the weighted total out of 100.

| Criterion | Weight | What a high score means |
|---|---:|---|
| Portfolio value | 25 | Strongly advances the portfolio and desired artistic positioning |
| Artistic potential | 20 | Clear opportunity for exceptional composition, detail and execution |
| Technical challenge | 15 | Meaningful technical stretch without unreasonable execution risk |
| Visual impact | 15 | Strong finished-piece impact and photographic potential |
| Originality | 10 | Distinctive concept rather than a generic or repetitive subject |
| Client commitment | 10 | Clear brief, realistic expectations, responsiveness and commitment to the process |
| Feasibility | 5 | Suitable placement, size, schedule and scope |

**Formula:** `(Portfolio × 25 + Artistic × 20 + Technical × 15 + Impact × 15 + Originality × 10 + Commitment × 10 + Feasibility × 5) ÷ 5`

## Rating anchors

- **5 — Exceptional:** clearly exceeds the standard expected for this criterion.
- **4 — Strong:** compelling and well supported.
- **3 — Suitable:** meets the required standard.
- **2 — Weak:** material concerns or limited upside.
- **1 — Poor:** does not meet the minimum requirement.

## Decision bands

| Score | Recommendation |
|---:|---|
| 85–100 | Priority |
| 70–84.99 | Consider |
| 55–69.99 | Reserve |
| Below 55 | Decline |

These bands are decision support, not an automatic selection rule.

## Portfolio balancing

After scoring, review the proposed ten as a complete portfolio. Avoid selecting ten similar projects simply because they score highest individually.

Target mix:
- 3 quarter sleeves, 4 half sleeves, 3 full sleeves.
- Strong black & grey realism representation.
- At least two colour or hybrid projects.
- At least two illustrative or signature-led projects.
- Variety in subject, composition, texture and visual storytelling.

Do not lower the quality threshold just to fill all ten places. Keep suitable applicants in reserve and reopen applications if necessary.

## Selection workflow

1. Check minimum requirements: 18+, viable placement, realistic brief and clear commitment.
2. Score each application independently against all seven criteria.
3. Record one or two sentences of evidence for each low or high score.
4. Rank by weighted total.
5. Review the portfolio mix and remove duplication.
6. Confirm feasibility and scope before offering a place.
7. Mark selected only after the applicant accepts the project terms.
8. Create the booking only after the written agreement and deposit arrangement are complete.

## Commercial guardrail

The selected fixed price includes additional time needed to complete the agreed scope. Before accepting a project, assess how uncertain the scope and execution time are. If the brief is likely to expand or cannot be bounded clearly, do not accept it as a fixed-price project until the scope is clarified.

Track actual tattooing hours against the minimum estimate. A high portfolio score does not automatically make an economically unsuitable project acceptable.
