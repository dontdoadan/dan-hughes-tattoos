## 4. What the fixed price includes

The fixed price covers the agreed design direction, placement and coverage described in section 2. It also includes additional tattooing time reasonably required by the artist to complete that original agreed scope to the intended finish.

The minimum time estimates used for campaign planning are:
- Quarter sleeve: 1.5 days.
- Half sleeve: 3 days.
- Full sleeve: 6 days.

These are estimates, not a cap on the time included in the fixed price. The client will not be charged extra solely because the original agreed scope requires additional tattooing time.

## 5. Changes outside the agreed scope

The fixed price does not automatically include:
- Additional body coverage or a larger tattoo area.
- A different body area or placement.
- Replacing the agreed subject or concept.
- Substantial client-requested changes after design approval.
- Separate tattoos or additional designs not included in section 2.

If the client requests a change outside the agreed scope, the artist will explain the impact and provide a written variation and price. No additional work or charge will proceed without the client's agreement.

## 6. Deposit and payment schedule

A 50% deposit is required to secure the project and must be paid at least seven days before the first session, unless both parties agree otherwise in writing.

The remaining balance may be paid in agreed instalments during the project. Every payment is credited against the fixed project price. The total amount payable for the agreed scope will not exceed the fixed price stated in section 3.

## 7. Cancellation and rescheduling

The artist will provide the client with the applicable cancellation and rescheduling terms before taking the deposit. Any amount retained following cancellation or a missed appointment must be fair, proportionate and compliant with applicable UK consumer law. The terms must not create an unfair penalty or permit the artist to retain more than is legally justified.

**Do not insert an automatic blanket forfeiture clause without legal review.** The previous 72-hour cancellation/no-show policy requires solicitor review before it is applied to this fixed-price offer.

## 8. Design process and approval

Design work begins after the deposit has been received, unless otherwise agreed in writing. The client is responsible for reviewing the design direction and raising concerns before approval. The artist will confirm when the design and scope are ready for the first session.

Changes after approval may constitute a scope variation under section 5.

## 9. Client responsibilities

The client confirms that they are at least 18 years old, will provide accurate information, will attend appointments on time, will follow the artist's preparation and aftercare guidance, and will raise any relevant safety or suitability questions before the session. Any health-related screening is handled separately and securely; do not put medical details into the campaign application form.

The artist may postpone or stop a session where reasonably necessary for safety, suitability or legal compliance.

## 10. Photography and portfolio use — separate permission

Applying for or booking a portfolio project does not grant permission to photograph, film, publish or market the client's tattoo or likeness.

Any permission must be recorded separately, with:
- The material that may be captured.
- Whether the client's face, name or other identifying features may appear.
- The channels permitted (website, Instagram, Facebook, print, advertising, etc.).
- Whether the permission is time-limited or ongoing.
- How the client can contact the artist to withdraw permission for future use, subject to any lawful limitations.

Declining marketing/image permission must not be treated as automatic consent. Obtain separate, explicit agreement.

## 11. Completion

The project is considered complete when the artist has completed the agreed tattoo scope in section 2. The fixed price covers the additional tattooing time needed to complete that scope. Any later request for additional coverage or a materially different design is a new scope discussion.
