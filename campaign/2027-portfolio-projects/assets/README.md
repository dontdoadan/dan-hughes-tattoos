- `slide-02-pricing.svg` — 4:5 selected project pricing.

## Export for social

Instagram and Facebook feed uploads should be exported as PNG or JPG. Import the SVG into Canva or another vector-capable design tool, export at 1080 × 1350 (feed) or 1080 × 1920 (Story), and inspect the export on a phone before posting.

These are typographic support assets, not evidence of completed tattoo work. Pair the launch post with a genuine, permissioned tattoo photograph from Daniel's portfolio. The campaign page's project cards are clearly labelled as concept directions.
