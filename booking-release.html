A clear process

From your idea to an agreed appointment.

01 / Enquire

Tell me the idea.

Share the tattoo concept, placement, approximate size and any reference links. Smaller work or an ongoing sleeve are both welcome.

02 / Agree the plan.

Choose the sessions.

We confirm project suitability, expected sessions and dates. For longer projects, the agreed £300 day rate applies to qualifying full-day sessions.

03 / Confirm

Reserve with £100.

Once we've agreed the details, your £100 project deposit confirms the booking. The deposit is credited towards the final session.

Planning more than one session?

One £100 project deposit carries forward across the agreed session plan. Each interim full-day session costs £300 on the day; the final full-day session costs £200 on the day after the £100 deposit credit. The actual number of sessions depends on your design and is agreed before booking.

Before you book

Clear terms. No surprises.

Who it is for
Adults aged 18 or over. Photo ID and normal tattoo consent/suitability checks are required before any session.
When a date is held
An enquiry is not a reservation. A session is confirmed after the project and date are agreed, applicable information is supplied, and the £100 deposit payment is received.
Preparation
The first session must normally be booked at least seven days after deposit payment to allow appropriate design preparation.
Changes and cancellations
Please give at least 72 hours’ notice. If you cancel or miss an appointment at shorter notice, some or all of the deposit may be retained only to the extent fair and reasonably necessary to cover unrecovered loss, accounting for any replacement booking and applicable consumer cancellation rights. Changes will be handled case by case.
Project scope
The £300 rate is for agreed full-day sessions under this release. Smaller tattoos are quoted individually; not every piece requires a full day. A deposit is credited once, not deducted from every session.
Location
Private, appointment-only tattooing in South London. Exact appointment location and requirements are confirmed during booking.
Have a project in mind?

Describe it first. No payment is taken by the enquiry form.
