THE BOOKING PROCESS

GOOD WORK
STARTS HERE.

Every project begins with an enquiry. We discuss the concept, placement, scale, schedule and suitability before a date is confirmed.

01 / ENQUIRE

Tell me about your idea.

Share the subject, placement, approximate size, references and what you want the tattoo to achieve. Submitting the form does not reserve a date.

02 / PLAN

Agree the project.

We'll discuss the scope, likely session requirements, pricing and suitable dates. Design preparation begins after the booking deposit has been received.

03 / CONFIRM

Secure your appointment.

A 50% deposit is required to confirm a booking and must be paid at least 7 days before the first session. For multi-session projects, the rolling deposit is deducted from the final session; interim sessions are paid on the day. Deposits are forfeited for cancellations or no-shows within 72 hours.

Ready to discuss a project?

Send an enquiry and we'll take it from there.
