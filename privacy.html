Your information / Your rights

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This notice explains how Daniel Hughes Tattoos handles information you share when making a tattoo enquiry through danhughestattoos.co.uk.

Who is responsible?

Daniel Hughes, operating Daniel Hughes Tattoos, is responsible for managing the personal information submitted through the website. Contact: dontdoadan@icloud.com.

What we collect

We collect the name and contact details you provide; your tattoo idea, preferred body placement, project size and approximate schedule; any optional reference links and budget preferences; your preferred contact method; your confirmation that you are over 18; and optional marketing preferences. We may also record limited technical and campaign-source data to help protect the form against abuse and understand how enquiries reach the website.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information or medical details in your initial tattoo brief. Any relevant health or consent information required for a tattoo session will be handled separately.

How and why we use it

We use your details to review and reply to your enquiry, discuss a potential commission, and manage any agreed booking. This is generally necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a service agreement. We also have a legitimate interest in securing the website and understanding enquiry performance. Optional promotional communications require your separate opt-in, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where data is kept

Website and database service providers, including Vercel and Supabase, provide technical hosting and storage. Enquiry records are restricted to authorised backend access and are not made publicly readable. We do not sell your information. Information may be processed by providers and sub-processors in other jurisdictions, subject to their applicable transfer safeguards.

How long we retain information

Enquiry details are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage ongoing correspondence and any booking, or comply with relevant legal and record-keeping requirements. You can request deletion of an inactive enquiry by contacting the email address above, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of your personal information; object to certain processing; withdraw optional marketing consent; or raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office. Send requests to dontdoadan@icloud.com.

Is this an appointment booking?

No. A submitted enquiry does not reserve a tattoo session or create any payment obligation. Booking details and any deposit are agreed separately.
