The process

A GOOD PROJECT
STARTS WITH A PLAN.

No rushed decisions and no off-the-shelf designs. We work through the idea, agree the direction and plan the sessions around the piece.

01

Tell me what you have in mind

Share your idea, placement, references and what matters most to you. You do not need to arrive with a finished design.

02

Shape the design

We discuss composition, scale and the practical details before agreeing the right approach for your tattoo.

03

Make time for the work

Once the project and dates are agreed, your sessions are planned so the work can be completed with care.
