LOOK AFTER YOUR TATTOO

CARE FOR
THE DETAIL.

Good aftercare supports the healing process. Follow the specific instructions provided at your appointment, as dressing type, placement and individual circumstances can affect what is appropriate.

Follow your artist's instructions

Keep the original dressing on for the period advised for the dressing used. Do not remove or replace specialist film earlier than instructed unless there is a problem or you have been advised to do so.

Keep it clean

When instructed to remove the dressing, wash your hands first. Gently clean the tattoo with lukewarm water and a mild, fragrance-free cleanser, then pat dry with clean disposable paper. Avoid scrubbing, picking or scratching.

Support healing

Use only a small amount of suitable aftercare product if advised. Avoid soaking the tattoo, swimming, saunas, direct sun and friction while it heals. Do not pick flakes or scabs.

When to seek advice

Some tenderness, redness and fluid during early healing can occur. Seek medical advice promptly if redness spreads, pain or swelling worsens, the area becomes hot, you develop a fever, or you notice pus or other signs of infection. For urgent or severe symptoms, use NHS 111 or emergency services as appropriate. Aftercare information on this page is general and is not a substitute for medical advice.

Need help?

If you are unsure about your dressing or aftercare instructions, contact the studio for guidance. For symptoms that may indicate infection, contact a healthcare professional.
