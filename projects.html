The principles

What makes these
projects different.

01 / Selected work

Projects are chosen for creative potential, suitability and commitment. An enquiry is not a guaranteed place.

02 / A complete plan

We discuss the subject, placement, approximate session requirements, price and deposit before anything is booked. Larger tattoos are planned as multi-session projects.

03 / Extra care, no shortcuts

For accepted portfolio projects only: where expressly agreed in the written project terms, extra sessions I decide are needed to finish or refine the agreed design to my standard are included at no additional session charge. Changes to the agreed scope are handled separately.

04 / A committed collaboration

You'll need to attend agreed sessions, follow aftercare instructions and allow sufficient healing time. Any photography or marketing use is discussed separately and requires your permission.

Common questions

The finer details.

Is the project free or unlimited?

No. Every project has an individually agreed price and scope. Any additional sessions included at my cost apply only when explicitly stated in the selected project's written terms and needed for the agreed design, not when the design is expanded at your request.

Do I need an exact tattoo design before applying?

No. Your starting idea, theme and placement are enough. We can shape the creative direction together.

When is a booking confirmed?

After the project is accepted, terms and date are agreed, and the required deposit is paid. Submitting an enquiry does not hold a date.

How many sessions will I need?

This depends on the size, complexity and placement. We'll discuss an initial plan before booking, with appropriate time allowed for healing between sessions.
